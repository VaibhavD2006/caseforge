## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic` / `polished` blend
- Creative direction: premium consulting SaaS launch — serious, aspirational, conversion-focused. Make it look expensive.
- Interpretation: Long-enough holds for every text element to be read. Big reveals on dark backgrounds. The leaderboard scene is the emotional peak — treat it like a hero reveal. Motion is fluid and intentional.
- Angle: The product's competitive layer (leaderboard) is the emotional climax — completing the arc from "you're practicing" to "you're actually winning."
- Hook: CaseForge wordmark on deep dark-green, clean and confident — product at rest before it does its thing.
- Outro: "Get the offer." — quiet, final, the product has already made its case.
- Avoid: Generic SaaS language, abstract filler, anything that could belong to a different product.
